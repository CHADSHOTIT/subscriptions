Scoring rules

Each request gets a score out of 100. Change the weights to match what your organisation values, and the queue and week plan update straight away.

What matters most

0 = ignore, 10 = counts most

Priority bands

A score at or above a band's minimum lands in that band. Anything below P3 goes to P4 Backlog.

How a score is worked out

  1. Each factor is rated 1 to 5. Text answers like "High" or "Whole organisation" are converted for you.
  2. Urgency uses whichever is higher: what the requester said, or how close the delivery date is (within a week counts as 5).
  3. Effort is flipped into Ease, so quick wins rise up the list.
  4. The weighted average becomes a score out of 100. Regulatory or mandatory requests get a boost of points on top.
  5. Missing answers count as a 3 and the request is marked Needs info so you know who to chase.

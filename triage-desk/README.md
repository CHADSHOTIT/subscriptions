# Triage Desk

A priority tracker and week planner for change requests.

- **Queue**: import your intake form export (.xlsx, .xls or .csv from Microsoft Forms, Google Forms or a tracker). Each request is scored out of 100 and placed in a band: P1 Do now, P2 Plan next, P3 Schedule in, P4 Backlog.
- **Week plan**: your open requests are laid out as a timetable. High priority work goes in the mornings, your own time is protected, and lower priority work fills the afternoons. Anything that would miss its delivery date is listed under "Needs a decision".
- **Scoring rules**: change the weights and the band cut-offs, export the ranked list, or download an intake form template.

Open `index.html` in a browser to use it standalone (data is kept in that browser). `app.html` is the same page in the form the claude.ai artifact publisher expects.
